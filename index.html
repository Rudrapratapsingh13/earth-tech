About Earth Tech

Bridging the gap between ecological design and asset longevity.

The built environment accounts for nearly 40% of annual global greenhouse gas emissions. A major portion is embodied carbon — emissions generated during material extraction, manufacturing, and construction.

Premature building demolition due to deferred maintenance or poor material selection is an ecological catastrophe. Earth Tech provides intuitive tools for property owners, designers, and forward-thinking builders to design durable structures that sequester carbon, endure shifting climates, and stay operational for over a century.
